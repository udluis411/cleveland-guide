LULU'S CLEVELAND GUEST GUIDE
Ohio City, Cleveland

ABOUT OHIO CITY
Ohio City is a diverse neighborhood just west of downtown Cleveland. It is the center of much economic redevelopment over the last 20 years and continues to grow. The residents are community oriented and very friendly. Block clubs are well established — if you are visiting, feel free to say hello to neighbors!

GETTING AROUND

No car needed for most things — almost everything great is within a 10-15 minute walk
Downtown (Guardians, Cavs, Browns games, concerts, conventions) is a 5 minute Uber, 25 minute walk, or quick public transit ride
The Rapid Station nearby connects to the airport, downtown, and University Circle
RTA bus is also available
Lime scooters are around the neighborhood for easy navigation
Bike rentals: Ohio City Bicycle Co-op (1840 Columbus Rd) — Wed noon–8pm by appointment, Thu–Sat noon–5pm. Book 2+ weeks ahead, provide rider heights 1 week ahead, valid ID and deposit required. Includes helmet, lock and spare tube.
Bike tours: Cleveland Cycle Tours (2061 Gehring Ave) — guided tours covering West 25th Street, breweries, and the West Side Market

WEATHER & SEASONS
Cleveland has 4 seasons. Fall, winter, and even spring can be cold. Patio recommendations (Great Lakes Brewery, Nano Brew, Forrest City Brewery, Heck's Cafe) are best on warm weather days. Kayaking is a warm weather activity. Edgewater Park beach and concessions are open in summer.

THE ANCHOR: WEST SIDE MARKET (5 minute walk)
A century-old public market with vendors selling produce, meats, cheeses, ethnic specialty items and prepared food stands. Visit to shop for dinner ingredients, find something interesting to bring home, or just walk around and chat with the friendly vendors.

ARTS & CULTURE

Art Galleries & Spaces:
- Transformer Station — Contemporary art gallery in Hingetown showcasing world-class exhibitions. A must for art lovers. Pair with a meal at Amba, Rosy or Larder for a perfect Hingetown afternoon. (~15-20 min walk or short drive)
- Spaces — Dynamic contemporary art gallery in Hingetown showcasing innovative and experimental work from local and national artists. (~15-20 min walk or short drive)
- 78th Street Studios — A massive 170,000 sq ft maze of artist studios and galleries in Detroit Shoreway. Go on a Third Friday for gallery walk night. (short drive)
- Cleveland Museum of Art — World class museum with free general admission. One of the best free days out in the city. University Circle. (~15 min drive or Rapid train)
- Museum of Contemporary Art (moCa) — Dynamic contemporary art in a stunning six-sided black glass building. Free admission. University Circle. (~15 min drive or Rapid train)

Music Venues:
- Beachland Ballroom & Tavern — Cleveland's most beloved independent music venue, housed in a former Croatian social hall in the Collinwood neighborhood. Two stages — the intimate Tavern and the larger Ballroom. Eclectic lineup of local and national acts nearly every night. A true Cleveland institution. (~20 min drive)
- Music Box Supper Club — Dinner and a show on the waterfront in the Flats. Two stages, great views of downtown Cleveland and the Cuyahoga River. Blues, jazz, soul, Americana and rock. Voted Best Live Music Venue by Cleveland Magazine readers multiple times. (~10 min drive)
- The Agora — A legendary Cleveland rock institution at 5000 Euclid Ave. Two rooms — a 2,000 capacity theater and a 500 capacity ballroom — hosting big national touring acts. The hot-pink neon sign is a Cleveland icon. (~15 min drive)
- Grog Shop — Intimate, gritty, beloved venue in Cleveland Heights known for indie rock, punk and alternative. Capacity 400, great for catching emerging artists up close. A favorite of true music fans. (~20 min drive)

Performing Arts:
- Playhouse Square — The largest performing arts district in the country outside of New York City. Broadway shows, concerts, comedy and more. Just a 5 min Uber from Ohio City.
- Cleveland Public Theatre — Nationally recognized contemporary theater in Detroit Shoreway. (~10 min drive)
- Severance Hall — Home of the world-renowned Cleveland Orchestra. One of the most beautiful concert halls in the country. University Circle. (~15 min drive)

NEIGHBORHOOD SPOTLIGHTS
Worth exploring beyond Ohio City — all reachable by car or rideshare
TREMONT (~10 min drive)
Tremont is Ohio City's neighbor to the south and one of Cleveland's most beloved neighborhoods. Less than a mile southwest of downtown, it's home to some of the best restaurants in the city with a distinctly artistic, bohemian feel — historic churches, eclectic art galleries, and great bars line the streets. Professor Avenue is the main drag for dining and nightlife. LuLu's favorites here are Fat Cats, Barrio, and Juneberry Table — but there's much more to discover. Civilization is a comfortable café in a beautiful historic 19th-century building, and the neighborhood is dotted with independent boutiques and galleries. Great for a dedicated dinner evening out.
DETROIT SHOREWAY & GORDON SQUARE (~10 min drive)
Just west of Ohio City along Detroit Avenue, this neighborhood is one of Cleveland's great urban revival stories. Home to more than 90 small businesses and 50-plus galleries and artisans, Gordon Square Arts District is a walkable stretch with unique cultural and dining opportunities. Highlights include the Capitol Theatre (a beautifully restored historic movie house), Cleveland Public Theatre, and Near West Theatre. The 78th Street Studios is a 170,000 sq ft maze of artist studios and galleries — worth a visit on a gallery day. Happy Dog is right in this neighborhood. Great for an arts evening, live theater, or a weekend afternoon browse.
DOWNTOWN CLEVELAND (5 min Uber / 25 min walk)
You're incredibly close to downtown — one of the best things about staying in Ohio City. The big draws are the sports venues (Progressive Field for the Guardians, Rocket Arena for the Cavs, Huntington Bank Field for the Browns) and the iconic lakefront attractions including the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame and the Great Lakes Science Center. Playhouse Square is the largest performing arts district in the country outside of New York City — worth checking for shows during your stay. East 4th Street is the go-to for pre-game dining and nightlife. Getting there is easy — Uber, the Rapid, or even a scenic 25-minute walk across the Hope Memorial Bridge.
UNIVERSITY CIRCLE (~15 min drive or Rapid train)
Cleveland's cultural crown jewel. Home to the Cleveland Museum of Art, Severance Hall (home to the world-renowned Cleveland Orchestra), Case Western Reserve University, the Cleveland Museum of Natural History, the Cleveland Botanical Garden, and the Museum of Contemporary Art — all packed into roughly one square mile. The Cleveland Museum of Art offers free general admission to its permanent collection, making it one of the best free days out in the city. You can take the RTA Rapid directly from the station near Ohio City — no car needed. Pair a museum visit with dinner in adjacent Little Italy for a perfect full-day itinerary.
LITTLE ITALY (~20 min drive or Rapid train)
One of Cleveland's most enduring ethnic neighborhoods, established in the late 19th century by Italian immigrants. Today it's a wonderful evening destination — stroll Mayfield Road, have pasta, grab a cannoli. More than 20 restaurants line the neighborhood along with art galleries and boutique shops. If you're visiting in August, the annual Feast of the Assumption transforms the neighborhood into a vibrant street festival celebrating Italian culture with food, live music, and community. Easily combined with a University Circle museum visit — they're right next to each other.
